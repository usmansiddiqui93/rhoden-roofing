Cedar Roof Myths: Separating Fact from Fiction

Mar 4, 20254 min readBy Rhoden Roofing
Residential RoofingRoof TypesWood Roofs

The questions keep coming in, and we’re here to discuss them. Homeowners continue to ask whether cedar roofing is too expensive, too fragile, or too high-maintenance. With so much conflicting information out there, it’s easy to fall for the common misconceptions about cedar roofs.

At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we believe in honest answers and real solutions. In this blog, we’re tackling cedar roof myths head-on to separate fact from fiction. If you’re considering a cedar roof, we’ll cover the real cedar roofing facts and benefits so you can make an informed decision. Whether you live in Wichita or the surrounding areas, understanding the truth about cedar roofing will help you decide if it’s the right choice for your home.

Myth #1: Cedar Roofs Don’t Last Long

The Truth About Cedar Roof Durability

A common misconception about cedar roofs is that they don’t last as long as other roofing materials. Some homeowners assume that because cedar is a natural material, it will deteriorate quickly. The truth is that a properly installed and maintained cedar roof can last 30 to 50 years, sometimes even longer.

What Makes Cedar Roofs So Durable?

Cedar contains natural oils that repel moisture, insects, and decay, making it more resistant to the elements than many synthetic materials. Treated cedar shingles offer even greater durability, with fire-resistant and preservative treatments available. Proper installation and ventilation also play a critical role in extending the lifespan of a cedar roof. With expert craftsmanship, a cedar roof can outlast many asphalt roofing systems.

Myth #2: Cedar Roofs Are High-Maintenance

How Much Maintenance Does a Cedar Roof Require?

It’s true that cedar requires some maintenance, but it’s not as demanding as many homeowners think. A properly installed cedar roof requires about the same level of upkeep as any other high-quality roofing material.

Simple Ways to Maintain a Cedar Roof

Routine inspections help catch small issues before they turn into costly repairs. Clearing debris and checking for moss or mildew buildup prevents premature wear. Applying protective treatments every few years can also help preserve the wood’s natural beauty and durability. If maintenance is a concern, Rhoden Roofing LLC offers professional cedar roof inspections and care to keep your roof in top condition.

Myth #3: Cedar Roofs Are Not Energy Efficient

The Insulating Power of Cedar Roofing

Many people assume that wood shingles won’t provide the same insulation as other materials, but that’s far from the truth. Cedar is a natural insulator, helping homes stay cooler in the summer and warmer in the winter.

How Cedar Roofing Saves Energy

Cedar absorbs and releases moisture efficiently, reducing temperature fluctuations inside your home. Because cedar shingles help regulate indoor temperatures, homeowners often see a decrease in heating and cooling costs over time. This makes cedar roofing an energy-efficient and environmentally friendly option.

Myth #4: Cedar Roofs Are Too Expensive

The Cost vs. Value of Cedar Roofing

There’s no denying that cedar roofs come with a higher upfront cost than asphalt shingles, but they also provide long-term value that outweighs the initial investment.

Cedar Roof Benefits That Justify the Cost

Cedar roofs last significantly longer than asphalt, which often needs replacement every 15 to 20 years. Cedar’s durability means fewer roof repairs and replacements over time. A cedar roof enhances a home’s curb appeal and resale value, making it an attractive investment for potential buyers. Cedar’s insulation properties can lead to lower energy bills, providing ongoing savings.

Rhoden Roofing LLC specializes in expert installation using high-quality cedar materials, ensuring homeowners get the most out of their investment.

Myth #5: Cedar Roofs Are a Fire Hazard

Are Cedar Roofs Safe?

Many homeowners worry that cedar roofs pose a fire risk. While untreated wood is naturally more flammable, modern cedar shingles are often treated with fire-resistant coatings, making them just as safe as other roofing options.

Fire-Resistant Cedar Roofing Options

Class A fire-rated cedar shingles offer the highest level of fire protection. Flame-retardant treatments can be applied to enhance safety and meet local building codes. Proper installation, combined with adequate ventilation and fire-resistant underlayment, further reduces any fire risk.

For homeowners concerned about fire safety, Rhoden Roofing LLC can recommend the best fire-treated cedar shingles to ensure protection and compliance with safety standards.

Why Cedar Roofing is a Great Choice for Wichita Homes

Cedar roofing is more than just a beautiful, natural roofing option—it’s a long-lasting, energy-efficient, and environmentally friendly choice. Many homeowners in Wichita worry about extreme weather conditions, but cedar’s durability makes it an excellent option for handling the area’s hot summers and cold winters.

Recap: Cedar Roofing Facts vs. Myths

Cedar roofs last 30 to 50 years with proper care. Routine inspections and occasional treatments keep cedar roofs in peak condition. Cedar naturally insulates homes, leading to reduced energy costs. While the initial cost is higher, cedar roofs offer long-term savings and add to home value. Fire-treated cedar shingles meet modern fire safety standards.
