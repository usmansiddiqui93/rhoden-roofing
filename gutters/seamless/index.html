The Benefits of Seamless Gutters

One thing many customers love about seamless gutters is how strong they are. It doesn’t matter how bad the weather gets, a properly installed seamless gutter system will stay the course and avoid leakage, especially when compared with sectional gutters. This is because seamless gutters do not have miters that join the different pieces together.

Seamless means the gutter system has no seams. Without seams, there’s no potential for leaks. The more seams a system has the more likely the gutters will leak. Plus, sectional gutters tend to be made of weaker materials, which makes them more susceptible to leaking and damages.

If your seamless gutter system does need repairing down the road, this can be more difficult. Seamless gutter systems often need to be completely replaced if they need repaired. When compared with sectional gutters, this can be costly. However, a properly installed seamless gutter should not require repairs for more than a decade. 

Costs

From a cost perspective, seamless gutters tend to cost about the same as sectional gutters once you factor in overall costs. Sectional gutters require extra pieces, more maintenance, caulking, and a variety of tools. Plus, sectional gutters often do not last as long as seamless gutters. 

If you’re looking for a long lasting gutter solution, seamless gutters in Wichita are ideal. At Rhoden Roofing, we’re fans of seamless solutions and these gutter systems will ensure you avoid leaks for years to come.
