Roofing Systems We Install for McPherson Properties

No two buildings are the same, and neither are their roofs. Our team is trained and certified across the full range of commercial roofing systems.

TPO Membrane Roofing

TPO is a go-to for energy-conscious businesses in McPherson. It reflects heat, resists tears, and stands up to chemical exposure—ideal for warehouses and industrial properties.

PVC Roofing

Stronger than TPO in some environments, PVC resists chemicals and oil-based damage. It’s perfect for restaurants, manufacturing sites, and facilities that need serious waterproofing performance.

Metal Roofing Systems

Durable. Efficient. Long-term. Metal roofing is a smart move for commercial buildings that value structural integrity and minimal maintenance—especially in Kansas wind zones.

Asphalt Shingles for Steep-Slope Roofs

Multifamily properties and certain commercial buildings still benefit from architectural shingles. We install high-performance systems with Class A fire ratings and industry-backed warranties.

Commercial Roof Coatings

Roof coatings help extend the life of aging flat roofs without a full replacement. If your existing system is still structurally sound, coatings are a cost-effective way to protect your investment.

Commercial Roofing FAQs for Property Owners in McPherson, KS

If you own or manage a commercial property in McPherson, you’ve likely had questions about timelines, materials, maintenance, and roof performance. Here are the most common questions we get—and the answers we give—when commercial clients want clarity before making a decision.

What does a commercial roofing service include?

A commercial roofing service includes inspection, installation, repair, replacement, and maintenance tailored to your building’s structure and goals. At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we also handle storm assessments, insurance documentation, and system recommendations that align with your building’s long-term ROI.

How long does a commercial roof last in McPherson?

A commercial roof in McPherson typically lasts 20–30 years for TPO or PVC systems, and up to 40+ years for metal roofs. Factors like regular maintenance, proper installation, and local weather exposure will all affect the actual lifespan of your roof.

How can I tell if my commercial roof needs to be repaired or replaced?

You can tell if your commercial roof needs repair or replacement by assessing its age, leak history, surface damage, and energy performance. A professional inspection will provide a clear diagnosis and help avoid costly guesswork.

Are roof coatings a good option for extending the life of my roof?

Roof coatings are a good option for extending the life of a commercial roof that’s still structurally sound. They seal the surface against leaks, improve energy efficiency, and offer a cost-effective way to delay full replacement.

What’s the best roofing system for flat commercial buildings?

The best roofing system for flat commercial buildings in McPherson is typically TPO or PVC, thanks to their durability, reflectivity, and resistance to water infiltration. Our team helps you select the right option based on budget, usage, and building needs.

Can Rhoden Roofing LLC help with insurance claims for commercial storm damage?

Yes, Rhoden Roofing LLC can help with insurance claims for commercial storm damage by inspecting your roof, documenting the damage, and working directly with your insurance adjuster. Our team ensures you get accurate, detailed support from start to finish.
