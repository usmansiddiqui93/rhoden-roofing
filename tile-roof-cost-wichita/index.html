How Much You’ll Pay For A New Tile Roof In Wichita?

By Rhoden RoofingUpdated Feb 21, 20252 min read
BlogCostsWichita

You can expect to pay between $30,000 and $75,000 for a tile roofing replacement in Wichita. On average, roof tiles in Wichita cost $18 to $40 per square foot. The total cost of your tile roofing replacement project depends on the size of your home, the type of tiles you install, and the labor costs of your recommended Wichita roofing company.

The Cost Of Tile Roofing In Wichita

There are many reasons to choose tile roofing for your home. It provides you with unmatched durability and improved curb appeal; the material also serves as a protective barrier against the elements so that they don’t damage or destroy anything in your home. Unlike other types of roofing materials, such as asphalt shingles, which offer limited life spans due to their material nature, tiles can last upwards of 100 years. Because of all these added benefits, tile roofing systems are one of the more expensive roof options.

Roof Inspection

It’s vital to hire a professional roof inspector to inspect your Wichita rooftop before making any roof installation plans or decisions. This is because your roofing inspector will be able to clarify if your roofing structures are strong enough to carry a tile rooftop or if they need to be strengthened with added support.

Types of Tile Roofing

There is an assortment of tile roofing options for homeowners to choose from. Your tile roofing selection includes clay, ceramic, concrete, slate, and Spanish tiles. Each of these tile options offers homeowners varying benefits and advantages; for example, clay and ceramic tiles are available in rich red, orange, and brown hues that’ll add vibrance to every home, whereas concrete tiles have a more muted and rugged appearance and are the most affordable tile roofing option.

Installation

A tile roof installation takes about three days to complete and requires a professional tile installations specialist to complete the job accurately. Because these rooftops require a specialist, homeowners will pay more for their installation than other roofing materials.
