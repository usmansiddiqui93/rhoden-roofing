How Much Will My New Metal Roof Cost In Wichita?

May 20, 20243 min readBy Rhoden Roofing
BlogCostsWichita

Embarking on a home improvement project often involves careful consideration of both budget and long-term investment. When it comes to safeguarding your home with a new metal roof, understanding the associated costs is paramount. In this article, we delve into the intricacies of pricing for metal roofing installations, exploring the factors that influence expenses and providing insights into how Rhoden Roofing can help you navigate this important decision with confidence.

Typical Expenses for Installing a New Metal Roof in Wichita

In Wichita, metal roof installations typically range from $14,375 to $75,000, depending on factors like roof size and the type of metal used. Costs per square usually vary between $625 and $3,000. On average, residents of Wichita can anticipate investing around $35,000 for a metal roof.

Roof Size Impacts the Cost of New Metal Roof Installation

When considering a new metal roof installation in Wichita, the size of the roof is crucial in determining costs. Roofs are measured in squares, with each square equivalent to 100 square feet. While Rhoden Roofing provides precise measurements for an accurate estimate, homeowners can gauge installation costs overall by estimating their roof’s size based on their home’s footprint, multiplied by a pitch factor to adjust for slope (see What is a Pitch Multiplier). For typical steepness of roofs, multiplying by 1.2 will get you in the right ballpark.

Additional Considerations Affecting Metal Roof Installation Costs in Wichita

Metal Roofing Materials

The type of metal chosen significantly influences the overall cost of a roof, as it dictates material prices, durability, and installation complexity. Premium metals, like copper, often command higher prices compared to more common options such as steel or aluminum. Additionally, specialized installation techniques may be required for specialty metals, further impacting the overall project cost.

Roof Pitch

The pitch or slope of a roof is crucial in determining overall expenses because it influences installation complexity and material requirements. Higher pitches demand additional safety measures, labor, and specialized equipment, leading to increased costs compared to roofs with shallower slopes. Additionally, roofs with steeper pitches may require more underlayment and flashing, further contributing to elevated overall expenses.

Roof Height

The height of your roof impacts labor needs and safety precautions during installation. Taller roofs generally demand more time and effort to access and work on, leading to increased labor expenses. Additionally, taller roofs may require additional safety measures or specialized equipment, further raising the overall costs.

Roof Complexity

The total cost of roof replacement is also significantly influenced by the complexity of the roof. This complexity involves various factors including labor demands, material requirements, and installation challenges. Roofs with intricate designs may require additional time, specialized materials, and extra resources, resulting in increased overall expenses.

New Installation or Replacement

Because of the labor-intensive process involved in removing and disposing of old roofing materials, replacements typically incur higher costs. This phase entails careful dismantling and proper disposal methods, resulting in increased expenses compared to new installations.
