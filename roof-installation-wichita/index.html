When a New Roof Installation Makes Sense

A new roof installation is usually needed when roofing is being added to a new structure or when part of the home is changing enough to require a complete roof system. For Wichita homeowners, this may include:

  • Building a new home that needs a roof installed from the deck up.

  • Roofing a detached garage, workshop, shed, or outbuilding.
  • Updating an older roof area during a larger remodel.

Residential Roofing Systems We Install in Wichita

Rhoden Roofing installs several residential roofing systems for Wichita homes. Each material has its own strengths, cost range, maintenance needs, and installation requirements.

Asphalt Shingle Roof Installation

Asphalt shingles are one of the most common choices for Wichita homes because they balance cost, durability, style options, and repairability. They work well on many roof designs and can fit both older homes and newer construction.

Rhoden Roofing installs asphalt shingle systems with the details that affect performance, including underlayment, starter shingles, flashing, ridge caps, ventilation, and fastening. For homeowners comparing upgraded shingle options, GAF architectural shingle installation can provide a strong mix of appearance, wind performance, and manufacturer-backed warranty options when the full system qualifies.

Metal Roof Installation

Metal roofing can be a good choice for homeowners who want long-term durability, strong weather performance, and lower maintenance. It can work well on certain homes, garages, porches, and detached structures when the design and budget fit.

Proper installation matters with metal roofing. Fastening, flashing, expansion, roof transitions, and ventilation all need to be handled correctly so the system performs as intended.

Cedar Roof Installation

Cedar roofing adds natural character and a distinct finish to homes where the style fits. It can be a strong option for homeowners who want warmth, texture, and a more traditional look.

Cedar also comes with maintenance expectations. Rhoden Roofing helps homeowners decide whether cedar is a practical fit for the home, neighborhood, and Kansas weather exposure.

Tile Roof Installation

Tile roofs, often made from clay or concrete, are known for durability, low maintenance, and long service life. They can provide a premium look and strong weather resistance when installed on a home that can properly support the system.

Before recommending tile, Rhoden Roofing considers roof structure, weight, slope, flashing needs, and installation requirements.

Slate Roof Installation

Slate is one of the most durable residential roofing materials available. It offers a premium appearance and can last for decades when installed correctly on the right structure.

Because slate is a specialty roofing system, it requires experienced installers and careful planning. Rhoden Roofing helps homeowners understand whether slate fits the home’s structure, style, and budget.

Low-Slope Residential Roof Installation

Some Wichita homes have low-slope roof areas over porches, additions, garages, sunrooms, or modern roof sections. These areas cannot always be treated like standard steep-slope shingle roofs.

Low-slope residential roof installation requires the right material, drainage planning, flashing, and seam details. Rhoden Roofing can evaluate the roof area and recommend a system that fits the structure and intended use.

What Proper Roof Installation Includes

A dependable roof starts with the details beneath the surface. Before shingles, panels, or tile go on, the roof needs proper preparation for the home, the material, and the Kansas weather.

Proper residential roof installation Wichita homeowners can trust may include:

  • Checking the roof deck for soft spots, rot, or damaged sheathing.
  • Using underlayment that fits the roof slope and material.
  • Installing ice and water protection where needed.
  • Flashing chimneys, valleys, vents, walls, and roof transitions correctly.
  • Installing starter shingles, ridge caps, drip edge, and roof edges.
  • Using the right fastening pattern for the selected material.
  • Reviewing ventilation and drainage before the roof is finished.
  • Protecting the property, cleaning up, and completing a final review.

These details matter in Wichita. Wind, rain, heat, and attic moisture can expose weak installation, which is why choosing professional roof installers in Wichita, KS, is important.

Residential Roof Installation FAQs for Wichita Homeowners

Wichita homeowners often have questions about timing, materials, warranties, and what happens before a new roof is installed. These answers can help you understand when a new installation makes sense and what to expect from the process.

When does a Wichita home need a new roof?

A Wichita home may need new roof installation for new construction, additions, garages, porches, outbuildings, major remodels, or a full roofing system upgrade. If the issue involves an existing leak, storm damage, or aging roof, Rhoden Roofing can help determine whether repair, replacement, or installation is the right fit.

What roofing material is best for a new roof in Wichita?

The best roofing material for a new roof in Wichita depends on the home’s structure, slope, budget, style, and weather exposure. Asphalt shingles are common; metal can provide long-term durability; and premium systems like tile, slate, or cedar may suit certain homes.

Can Rhoden Roofing install a roof on a garage or addition?

Yes. Rhoden Roofing can install residential roofing on garages, additions, porches, outbuildings, and other roof areas when the structure is ready for roofing. Matching the slope, drainage, flashing, and material details to the existing home is important.

Do you inspect decking before installing a new roof?

Yes. Decking condition is an important part of residential roof installation. Soft, damaged, rotted, or uneven decking should be addressed before installing new roofing materials.

How long does residential roof installation take?

The timeline for residential roof installation depends on roof size, material type, roof complexity, weather, decking condition, and project scope. Rhoden Roofing explains the expected timing before work begins.

Does Rhoden Roofing offer warranty options for new roof installation?

Yes. Rhoden Roofing offers warranty options based on the roofing system, product selection, manufacturer requirements, and project eligibility. Rhoden also backs its workmanship with its Lifetime Workmanship Warranty.
