Construction Process

Our proven process takes every roof from the first on-site meeting to the final photo report in five steps, built to city code and manufacturer specs.

Get a Free EstimateCall (316) 927-2233
Proven process

Five steps, 25 checkpoints

Every step ends with something you can see: a photo checklist, a written quote, a confirmed schedule, a photo-documented build and a photo report for insurance.

  1. Step 1

    Needs Analysis

    • Initial on-site meeting
    • Gather all measurements
    • Complete photo checklist
    • Spotlight on your needs
    • Understand your goals
  2. Step 2

    Design & Presentation

    • Estimator builds quote
    • Roof designed to city code and manufacturer specs
    • Demonstration of “roofing system”
    • What to expect
    • Address your questions or concerns
    • Discuss timeline
  3. Step 3

    Behind the Scenes

    • Address any insurance supplements
    • Confirm design is to spec
    • Schedule your job
    • Order materials
  4. Step 4

    Production of Roof

    • Project manager introduction
    • On-site pre-construction meeting
    • Confirm scope of work
    • Ensure roof built to city code and manufacturer specs
    • Photo documentation
    • Ensure 100% customer satisfaction
  5. Step 5

    Finalization of Process

    • Create photo report for insurance
    • Assist you with insurance paperwork
    • Finalize any supplements and send invoice
    • Obtain your feedback
