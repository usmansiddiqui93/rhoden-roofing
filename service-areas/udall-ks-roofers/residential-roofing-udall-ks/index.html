Roof Replacement: Not Just a Patch Job—A Full Upgrade

Think of your roof like the foundation of your peace of mind. A solid roof means no leaks, no stress when storms hit, and no midnight buckets catching water in the living room.

Here’s how to know your roof is ready for replacement:

  • Shingles that look like they’ve been through a blender
  • Water stains on your ceiling (never a good sign)
  • Your energy bill keeps creeping up
  • That sinking feeling when you realize your roof is pushing 20+ years

When we do a roof replacement, we’re not cutting corners. We’re installing premium materials from top-tier brands—GAF, DaVinci, Malarkey—built to handle Udall’s unpredictable weather. And yes, it’s backed by a Lifetime Workmanship Warranty. Because that’s how confident we are in what we do.

Roof Repairs: Fix It Fast, Fix It Right

Sometimes it’s not about replacing the whole thing. Maybe it’s just one section that took a beating in last week’s storm. That’s where our roof repair services come in.

Our repair services include:

  • Shingle replacements
  • Leak detection (and permanent fixes, not temporary patches)
  • Storm and hail damage repair
  • Flashing, chimney, and vent issues
  • Gutter-related leaks

The point? If something’s wrong, we fix it the right way—the first time.

Roofing Materials: No Cookie-Cutter Solutions

We don’t do “one-size-fits-all.” Every home is different. Every homeowner has different needs. That’s why we offer multiple roofing material options that align with your goals, your budget, and your long-term plans.

Let’s talk options:

  • Asphalt Shingles – Affordable, durable, and available in a variety of styles.
  • Metal Roofing – For folks who want strength, energy efficiency, and decades of performance.
  • Tile & Slate – Classic, long-lasting, and adds serious curb appeal.
  • Cedar Shake – Natural beauty meets insulation benefits.
  • Flat Roofing – Perfect for certain modern builds or additions.

We’ll walk you through the pros and cons of each. No fluff, no upsell. Just solid advice based on what’s best for your home.
