Roof Inspections & Maintenance

Regular inspections are critical for identifying wear before it leads to major damage. We offer scheduled maintenance programs that help extend the lifespan of your roof while reducing repair costs over time.

  • Annual or semi-annual inspection plans
  • Drainage and flashing checks
  • Minor repair recommendations
  • Comprehensive condition reports for property managers

Commercial Roof Repair

If you’ve noticed signs of leaks, pooling water, or storm damage, it’s time to call in a professional commercial roofing company. Our repair crews respond quickly to minimize downtime and protect the interior of your building.

We repair:

  • Membrane punctures
  • Flashing separation
  • Leaks around HVAC units
  • Ponding and drainage issues
  • Wind or hail damage

Roof Replacement & Installation

If your commercial roof is beyond repair, we’ll guide you through the replacement process from start to finish. We offer a full selection of roofing systems designed for Kansas’s climate and commercial building types.

Roofing systems we install:

  • TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin)
  • EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer)
  • Modified Bitumen
  • Metal roofing systems
  • Flat and low-slope commercial roofs

We’ll evaluate your property and help you choose the most efficient, durable option for your building type and budget.
