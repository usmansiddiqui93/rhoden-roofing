How Long do Cedar Roofs Last in Wichita

Sep 27, 20243 min readBy Rhoden Roofing
Roof TypesWood RoofsWichita

Wichita’s climate is as dynamic as a rollercoaster ride—hot, dry summers sizzle into crisp, chilly winters, and let’s not forget the occasional spring storm. Amidst these weather whims, homeowners often find themselves pondering the durability of their roofing. At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we know that a cedar roof isn’t just a roof; it’s a statement. Cedar roofing brings a natural, rustic charm that’s hard to beat, and we’re here to break down just how long you can expect that beauty to last.

Why Cedar Roofing?

Cedar roofs are like the artisan cheese of roofing materials—distinctive, high-quality, and oh-so-appealing. They offer a warm, timeless look that stands out from the cookie-cutter asphalt shingles you see everywhere. At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we’re passionate about installing both cedar shake and cedar shingle roofs, each with its own flair.

Cedar Shake vs. Cedar Shingle: What’s the Difference?

Cedar shakes and cedar shingles are two types of cedar roofing that offer different vibes. Cedar shakes are the more rugged, hand-split option. They’re like the rough-hewn wood furniture of roofing—textured and charmingly imperfect. Shingles, on the other hand, are machine-cut and smoother, providing a more uniform appearance. They’re the polished, sleek counterpart to shakes.

Both types have a reputation for being tough as nails, but they come with different aesthetics and benefits. Cedar shakes can add a bit of a storybook feel to your home, while cedar shingles offer a more streamlined, classic look. Both types of cedar roofing can last quite a while with the right care, which is a pretty impressive lifespan for any roofing material.

How Long Can You Expect Cedar Roofs to Last?

Cedar roofs can usually last up to 25 years in Wichita’s climate. That’s right—whether you choose the rustic charm of cedar shakes or the refined elegance of cedar shingles, you’re investing in a roof that’s built to weather the storms and sunshine alike. The longevity of your cedar roof, however, isn’t just about the material; it’s also about how well you maintain it.

Maintaining Your Cedar Roof: The Secret to Cedar Roof Longevity

To ensure your cedar roof lasts as long as possible, it’s all about regular upkeep. Here are some tips to keep your cedar roof looking fantastic and functioning well:

Regular Inspections: Twice a year, ideally in the spring and fall, get up there (or better yet, call us!) to check for damage or wear. Look for missing or damaged shakes or shingles, and address issues promptly.

Keep it Clean: It’s smart to get rid of leaves and branches that can trap a lot of moisture and lead to things like mold or rot. A gentle hose-down can help keep your roof looking clean, but avoid high-pressure washing, which can damage the wood.

Remove Moss & Algae: These can grow on cedar roofs, particularly in shaded areas. If you spot them, treat the affected areas with a specialized cleaner.

Is it Properly Ventilated? Proper attic ventilation helps prevent moisture buildup, which can prematurely deteriorate your cedar roof. 

Let Rhoden Roofing LLC Take Care of Your Cedar Roof

A cedar roof isn’t just a roofing choice; it’s a lifestyle statement that enhances the beauty and value of your home. With proper installation and maintenance, it’s a roof that can last up to 25 years, standing strong against Wichita’s diverse climate. At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we’re experts in both cedar shake and cedar shingle roofing, and we’re here to ensure your roof not only lasts but thrives.
