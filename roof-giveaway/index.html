Rhoden Roofing’s 18th Christmas Giveaway

Every Christmas, Rhoden Roofing gives back to the Wichita Community by giving away a free roof to a deserving family or individual. The Roof Giveaway is one of the most important community projects we do each year, and definitely the one we look forward to the most. The Greater Wichita community has been so good to us since we started in 2008.

We are proud to give back to our community and show our appreciation by donating a roof to a deserving family or individual. Our team is honored to be able to help these recipients protect what matters most by staying safe and dry in their own home.

How It Works

Every Christmas (and throughout the year, see submission form below), we ask our community to help us find a deserving family or individual in need of a new roof. People here in the greater Wichita area are invited to nominate friends, relatives, neighbors, or even themselves. At the end of the nomination period, the submissions go into review, and we narrow down the field. In addition to the details of the roof/property in question, we are also looking for deserving recipients who demonstrate good character and community engagement. During the Spring, we announce our Christmas Roof Giveaway winner and are able to begin work.
