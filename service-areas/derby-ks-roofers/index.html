How long does a typical roof installation take?

The duration varies, but most installations are completed within a few days, weather permitting.

What is the average lifespan of a roof?

Asphalt shingle roofs typically last 20-30 years, while metal roofs can last 40-70 years or more.

What are the benefits of roof ventilation?

Proper ventilation extends roof lifespan by reducing heat buildup and moisture accumulation in the attic.
