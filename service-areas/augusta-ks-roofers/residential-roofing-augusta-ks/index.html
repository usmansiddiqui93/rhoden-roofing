Roof Installation and Replacement in Augusta

Installing a new roof is a significant investment, and our team is here to guide you through the process. We specialize in installing:

  • Asphalt shingles, which are affordable, durable, and available in various colors and styles to suit your home
  • Metal roofing, a long-lasting option with excellent energy efficiency
  • Tile and slate roofs, which provide timeless beauty combined with unmatched durability
  • Cedar shakes that offer rustic charm with natural insulating properties

Our installation process ensures your roof is properly ventilated, watertight, and designed to withstand Augusta’s weather challenges.

When Is It Time for a Replacement?

Signs your roof might need replacing include curling, cracked, or missing shingles, water leaks or stains inside your home, and damage from hail or strong winds. If you’re unsure, schedule a professional inspection with Rhoden Roofing LLC to assess your roof’s condition.

Expert Roof Repairs

Storms in Augusta can cause sudden damage to your roof. Our experienced contractors offer prompt repair services to address leaks and water infiltration, missing or damaged shingles, and flashing or gutter issues. We’ll restore your roof’s integrity quickly and efficiently, minimizing disruption to your home life.
