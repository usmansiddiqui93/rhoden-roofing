5 Common Causes of Commercial Roof Damage in Wichita

May 14, 20245 min readBy Rhoden Roofing
Commercial roofingCommercial Roof Inspection & MaintenanceWichita
Commercial roofs are a significant investment for any business, providing essential protection against the elements. However, despite their durability, commercial roofs in Wichita are susceptible to various types of damage. Understanding the common causes of this damage can help business owners take preventive measures and extend the lifespan of their roofing systems. In this blog, we will discuss five common commercial roof damage causes, the importance of timely commercial roof repairs, the expected lifespan of a commercial roof, and the best materials for commercial roofing available at Rhoden Roofing LLC. If you are facing roofing issues, contact us for a professional estimate and ensure your roof remains in top condition.

1. Weather Extremes

Wichita experiences a range of weather conditions, from hot summers to cold winters, and everything in between. These weather extremes can take a toll on commercial roofs. High temperatures can cause materials to expand, leading to cracks and other forms of deterioration. Conversely, freezing temperatures can cause materials to contract, potentially causing splits and breaks. Additionally, hailstorms can cause significant damage, especially to less durable roofing materials. Strong winds can lift and tear roofing materials, leaving the underlying structure exposed to further damage.

Preventive Measure: Regular maintenance and inspections can help identify and address weather-related damage early. At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we offer comprehensive maintenance plans to keep your roof in excellent condition year-round.

2. Poor Installation

One of the leading causes of commercial roof damage is poor installation. If a roof is not installed correctly, it can lead to a host of problems, including leaks, poor drainage, and structural weaknesses. Incorrect installation of flashing, inadequate sealing, and improper alignment of roofing materials can all contribute to premature roof failure.

Preventive Measure: Ensure your roof is installed by experienced and certified professionals. Rhoden Roofing LLC is a GAF Master Elite Contractor, guaranteeing high-quality installation using the best practices in the industry.

3. Lack of Maintenance

Regular maintenance is crucial for the longevity of any commercial roof. Neglecting maintenance can lead to the accumulation of debris, clogged gutters, and undetected minor issues that can escalate into major problems. For instance, standing water due to blocked drainage can lead to water infiltration and damage to the roof’s structure.

Preventive Measure: Schedule regular maintenance checks with a trusted roofing contractor. Rhoden Roofing LLC offers tailored maintenance plans to suit your specific needs, ensuring potential issues are addressed before they become costly repairs.

4. Roof Traffic

Commercial roofs often accommodate HVAC systems, satellite dishes, and other equipment, which means there can be a significant amount of foot traffic. Improperly managed roof traffic can lead to punctures, tears, and other forms of damage, especially if heavy equipment is being transported across the roof.

Preventive Measure: Limit access to the roof and use designated walkways to minimize damage. Our team at Rhoden Roofing LLC can help design and install protective walkways and barriers to protect your roof from traffic-related damage.

5. Moisture and Leaks

Moisture is a commercial roof’s worst enemy. Water infiltration can occur due to a variety of reasons, including damaged flashing, punctures, or pooling water. Once moisture penetrates the roofing system, it can lead to mold growth, insulation damage, and structural issues.

Preventive Measure: Regular inspections and prompt repairs of any detected leaks are essential. Rhoden Roofing LLC specializes in roof repair services, addressing leaks and moisture issues efficiently to prevent further damage.

Importance of Timely Commercial Roof Repairs

Timely repairs are critical to maintaining the integrity of your commercial roof. Ignoring small issues can lead to significant damage, resulting in costly repairs or even the need for a complete roof replacement. Regular inspections and prompt attention to any signs of damage can save you money and extend the life of your roof.

Pro Tip: Don’t wait until you notice a leak or visible damage. Schedule regular inspections with Rhoden Roofing LLC to catch problems early.

How Long Should a Commercial Roof Last?

The lifespan of a commercial roof depends on various factors, including the type of material used, the quality of installation, and the level of maintenance. On average, a commercial roof can last between 20 to 45 years. Metal roofs, known for their durability, can last up to 45 years, while single-ply membrane roofs like PVC and TPO can last up to 30 years with proper maintenance.

Best Materials for a Commercial Roof

At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we offer a range of high-quality commercial roofing materials designed to withstand Wichita’s weather conditions and provide long-lasting protection. Here are the best materials we recommend:

1. Single-Ply Membrane Roofing

  • PVC (Poly Vinyl Chloride): Known for its durability and resistance to chemicals, PVC roofs are an excellent choice for commercial buildings with flat or low-slope roofs.
  • TPO (Thermo Plastic Polyolefin): TPO is a popular option due to its energy efficiency, UV resistance, and cost-effectiveness. It’s ideal for businesses looking to reduce their energy bills while maintaining a durable roof.

2. Commercial Metal Roofs

  • Metal roofs are renowned for their longevity, often lasting over 40 years. They are highly resistant to weather extremes and require minimal maintenance, making them a cost-effective option in the long run.
