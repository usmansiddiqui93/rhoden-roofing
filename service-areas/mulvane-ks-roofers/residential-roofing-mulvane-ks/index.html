Comprehensive Residential Roofing Services

Need a New Roof?

If your roof is outdated or showing signs of wear, a replacement may be the best option to protect your home. We specialize in installing high-quality roofing systems, including:

  • Asphalt shingles for affordability and versatility
  • Metal roofing for superior durability and energy efficiency
  • Tile and slate for timeless beauty and exceptional longevity
  • Cedar shakes for natural, rustic charm

We’ll help you select the ideal material to complement your home’s style and meet your needs. Our installation process is thorough, ensuring your roof not only looks great but provides unmatched performance for years to come.

Storm Damage Repairs

Kansas storms can cause damage that requires immediate attention. Whether it’s missing shingles, leaks, or structural issues, Rhoden Roofing LLC provides fast, reliable repair services. Our team quickly identifies and resolves problems, ensuring your roof is restored to peak condition. Timely repairs prevent minor issues from turning into expensive, long-term damage.

Roof Coatings for Added Protection

A roof coating can extend the life of your existing roof, adding durability and energy efficiency while enhancing waterproofing. This cost-effective solution is perfect for homeowners looking to avoid a full replacement. Coatings also help protect against UV damage, making them a great option for Kansas homes exposed to harsh sunlight.

Gutter Installation

Proper drainage is essential to protect your home’s foundation and landscaping. We install gutters that seamlessly complement your roof, ensuring water is directed away from your home to prevent costly water damage. Quality gutters can also improve the overall efficiency of your roofing system.
