Roof Replacement: A Smart Investment for Your Home

Roofs don’t last forever, and waiting too long to replace one can lead to bigger issues like leaks, mold, and structural damage. A full roof replacement restores your home’s protection, improves energy efficiency, and boosts curb appeal.

Signs It’s Time for a Roof Replacement

  • Cracked, curled, or missing shingles
  • Leaks or water stains inside your home
  • Moss or algae growth on shingles
  • Shingles shedding granules into gutters
  • Roof is over 20 years old and showing signs of aging

We use high-quality materials from leading manufacturers like GAF, DaVinci, and Malarkey, ensuring durability and long-term performance.

Roof Repairs: Fixing Problems Before They Get Worse

If your roof has storm damage or small leaks, timely repairs can prevent bigger, more expensive problems. Our team provides quick, effective repairs to keep your home protected.

Common Roofing Repairs We Handle

  • Storm damage repairs (hail, wind, and debris impact)
  • Shingle and flashing replacements
  • Leak detection and waterproofing
  • Gutter and ventilation adjustments

We conduct a thorough inspection before any repair to make sure we address the root cause of the problem—not just the symptoms.
