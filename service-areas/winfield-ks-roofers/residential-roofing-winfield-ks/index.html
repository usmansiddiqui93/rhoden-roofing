Roof Replacement: Get Ahead of the Problem

Waiting until you have an active leak to replace your roof? That’s a rookie move. It’s also how people end up with mold, wood rot, and interior repairs that cost 5x more than the roof itself.

A professionally installed roof replacement not only solves those issues—it also adds resale value, boosts curb appeal, and gives you peace of mind every time the weather turns.

Signs It’s Time for a Roof Replacement

  • Your roof is 20+ years old
  • Shingles are missing, cracked, or curling
  • You’re spotting dark streaks or moss growing on the surface
  • You’ve had more than one leak in the last few years
  • Your energy bills are creeping up (yes, poor roofing affects insulation)

We only use top-rated materials from manufacturers like GAF, DaVinci, and Malarkey. These aren’t big-box brands—they’re premium systems built for longevity, especially in climates like Winfield’s.

Every install comes with our Lifetime Workmanship Warranty. You won’t find a better guarantee than that, because we believe your investment should come with accountability.

Metal Roofing – Efficiency + Durability

Tile & Slate – Premium Style, Maximum Strength

Flat Roofing – For Modern Builds and Low-Slope Designs

Perfect for certain home additions or contemporary designs. Our flat roofing systems are engineered for durability and proper drainage.
