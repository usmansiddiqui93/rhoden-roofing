Benefits of Aluminum Roofing

There’s a number of reasons both homeowners and commercial properties opt for aluminum as their roofing material of choice. Let’s take a look at some of them.

Temperature Regulation

Aluminum is one of the best temperature regulators out there. It can reflect almost 100% of the rays from the sun, meaning that your house will stay cool even when it’s hot outside, while reflecting heat back toward the inside of your house, so it stays warmer in the winter.

Traditionally, the roof is where you lose cold and hot air, so an aluminum roof is a great barrier to keep your home at an optimal temperature, reducing the need for heaters or coolers.

Affordability

Aluminum will have an impact on your energy bills, saving you money in the long run. In addition to that, its light weight means that you will save on labor costs at installation too. When calculating the lifespan of an aluminum roof against its initial cost and the many benefits it offers, it is a worthy investment.

Weather Resistance and Durability

Aluminum is durable enough to last a long time. It can withstand extreme weather like most other metals without showing much wear and tear. High humidity, wind, acid rain, snow, sleet, hail, and other natural forces are no match for an aluminum roof. Because of its resistance to harsh weather, aluminum is an excellent choice for Wichita homes.

Malleability

One of the best features of aluminum is its malleability, meaning you can shape it to look like shingles, tile, or any other design you prefer. This is especially useful for homeowners keen on mimicking a more premium look at a fraction of the cost.

Environmentally Friendly

We’ve mentioned aluminum’s contribution to a more energy efficient home. In addition to that, it is made from many recycled materials, with some containing up to ninety percent recycled metal. Being a lightweight material it’s also easier for the aluminum roofing service provider to transport the materials, saving the environment from excess fuel consumption and pollution.
