Roof Replacement: Secure Your Home for the Next 30 Years

Replacing your roof is a big investment. We treat it with the seriousness it deserves. Whether your roof is past its prime, damaged in a storm, or just not performing like it should, a replacement can give you peace of mind and immediate value.

Signs It’s Time to Replace Your Roof

  • Your shingles are cracked, curled, or completely missing
  • There’s water damage on your ceilings or interior walls
  • You’re seeing mold or moss growing on your roof
  • Your roof is over 20 years old and constantly needing repairs

Here’s how we approach it: We assess your current roof, explain your options in plain English, and help you select a replacement that fits your home and your goals. We use elite-level materials from manufacturers like GAF, DaVinci, and Malarkey—because good enough isn’t good enough for us.

And yes, the install is covered by our industry-leading Lifetime Workmanship Warranty.

Roof Repairs: Fast Fixes, Done Right

A full replacement isn’t always necessary. Sometimes it’s one leak, one storm-damaged section, or one improperly installed vent causing the issue. Our repair team knows how to spot the real problem—and fix it right the first time.

Common Residential Roofing Repairs We Handle

  • Hail and wind damage from Kansas storms
  • Missing or torn shingles
  • Persistent leaks and water infiltration
  • Flashing, chimney, and ventilation issues
  • Gutter or drainage problems affecting roof performance

We don’t slap a patch on it and call it a day. We get in there, figure out the root cause, and solve it with materials and techniques that last.
