Advantages and Disadvantages of Metal Roofing

By Rhoden RoofingUpdated Dec 03, 20252 min read
Residential RoofingRoof TypesMetal Roofing

Nowadays, metal is a top choice for both home and business owners. Its weather resistance, durability, and longevity have made it an excellent material for environments of all kinds. In the past, homeowners have tended to avoid choosing metal roofing due to its reputation for being loud or dull; however, the material has significantly improved over time, making it a leader in the roofing industry.

If you’re thinking about installing a metal roofing system on your home, it’s essential for you to know what the roofing material offers you and what it doesn’t. This article will be detailing the advantages and disadvantages of metal roofing systems.

Advantages

Durability

Metal roofs are well known for their durability and sturdiness. They can withstand most weather conditions, protect homes from harmful UV rays, withstand wind speeds of up to 140 mph, and quickly shed significant amounts of snow. This roofing material is designed to last a lifetime despite harsh climates.

Low Maintenance

If maintenance is not something that you particularly enjoy, then a metal roofing system is perfect for you. The material’s durability makes it practically indestructible, requiring very little maintenance. If you’re worried about rust and oxidation, materials like steel and copper have a natural protective layer that prevents rust from permeating the material and breaking it down.

Versatility

Many homeowners are usually hesitant to install a metal roof because of the belief that the material is only available in one color and one design. However, thanks to its malleability, you’re able to install a metal shake, shingle as well as tile and slate imitation roofing systems. Your home can now be protected by the strength and durability of a metal roof while enjoying the aesthetic benefits of other more expensive roofing materials.

Disadvantages

Initial Cost

The initial cost of metal roofing might scare away homeowners who are used to installing asphalt shingles on their rooftops. Metal roofing systems can cost a pretty penny initially; however, they are more affordable in the long run. They have a lifespan of more than 50 years, and they require minimal maintenance, making them a cost-effective roofing system in the long term.

Require A Specialist

To get the most out of your metal roofing system and guarantee that it lasts longer, you would require a metal roof specialist to install your roofing system. Inexperienced or non-specialized workers may take shortcuts or not know what to do should a problem arise. Using an inexperienced worker may also impact your material’s warranty and insurance coverage on your new rooftop.

Denting

While metal is a highly durable material, if you live in an area that experiences golf ball-sized hail, chances are, over time, your roof may be covered with dents. But it’s important to note that any roofing system will suffer significant damage when exposed to hail that size, so rather a few dents than huge holes through your ceiling.
