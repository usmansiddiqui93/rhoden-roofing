Roof Replacement That Makes Sense

If your roof is 20+ years old, or it looks like it’s been through a prize fight, it’s time for a full replacement. And we’re not going to sugarcoat it—roof replacement is a big deal. It’s an investment. But done right, it’s one you only have to make once.

Here’s how you know it’s time to stop patching and start replacing:

  • Your shingles are curling, cracked, or straight-up missing
  • There are leaks—or even worse, mold
  • You see granules in your gutters (that’s your shingles breaking down)
  • You’ve had to call someone “just to patch it” more than once in the last 2 years

We use materials from top-tier manufacturers like GAF, Malarkey, and DaVinci. Why? Because quality matters—and it shows up on your energy bill, your resale value, and your peace of mind.

Materials That Match Your Home and Your Priorities

A good roof doesn’t just protect your house. It makes your house look better and work better. We help you pick the right material based on what actually matters to you—cost, longevity, aesthetics, and performance.

Here’s the rundown:

  • Asphalt Shingles – Affordable, reliable, and not “just builder grade.” We use the good stuff.
  • Metal Roofing – Looks sharp, lasts forever, and reflects heat. That’s a win-win-win.
  • Tile & Slate – Premium, timeless, heavy-duty. You want luxury? Start here.
  • Cedar Shake – Natural, beautiful, and surprisingly energy-efficient when installed correctly.
  • Flat Roofing – Modern, practical, and ideal for low-slope designs.
