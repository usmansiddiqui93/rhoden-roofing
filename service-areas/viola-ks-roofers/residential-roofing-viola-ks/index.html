Residential Roofing Services for Viola Homes

Your roof should be built for the long haul, not just until the next hailstorm rolls through. Whether you need a complete roof replacement or a few professional repairs to buy your current roof more years, we make sure the job is done right the first time.

Roof Replacement: Protecting What Matters Most

A roof replacement is more than just simply dealing with damage—it’s an investment in security and peace of mind. A well-installed roof improves energy efficiency, prevents water intrusion, and even boosts your property’s value.

Signs It’s Time to Replace Your Roof:

  • Your roof is 20+ years old
  • Shingles are cracked, curled, or missing.
  • You see damage after a storm.
  • Leaks or indoor moisture are becoming common.
  • You want better energy efficienc.y

We use premium materials from brands proven to perform against Kansas weather extremes. And because installation quality matters as much as the product, every roof replacement we handle is backed by our Lifetime Workmanship Warranty.

We don’t gamble with your roof—we build lasting protection.

Roof Repairs: Small Issues, Fixed Right

Sometimes it’s not about replacing everything—it’s about fixing the right thing. A small leak or a few damaged shingles can turn into bigger problems if ignored, which is why we provide targeted, professional repairs.

Our Repair Services Cover:

  • Storm, hail, and wind damage
  • Leaks and water stains
  • Missing or cracked shingles
  • Chimney and flashing repairs
  • Ventilation and drainage issues

We always begin with a detailed inspection to ensure we’re not just treating the symptoms—we address the root cause.

Roofing Materials to Fit Your Home & Goals
