Roof Replacement: A Smart Move, Not Just a Necessary One

Most homeowners wait too long to replace their roof. Then they’re stuck dealing with water damage, mold, and insulation issues—not to mention the added cost of repairing what the roof didn’t protect.

A proactive roof replacement gives you more than just peace of mind. It gives your home:

  • Improved energy efficiency
  • Better insulation
  • A sharp exterior upgrade
  • Protection that actually lasts

When Should You Replace Your Roof?

  • Your roof is 20 years or older
  • You’ve had repeated repairs and the issues keep coming back
  • You see shingle damage (missing, curling, cracking)
  • Your attic shows signs of leaks or poor ventilation
  • You’re planning to sell and want to increase your home’s value

At Rhoden Roofing LLC, we use top-tier materials from manufacturers like GAF, DaVinci, and Malarkey—brands chosen for their performance in climates just like Wellington’s. And everything is backed by our Lifetime Workmanship Warranty.

We don’t just install roofs. We deliver reliable systems built to perform for decades.

Roof Repairs: Small Fixes Done Right the First Time

Not every roof needs to be replaced. In fact, sometimes a simple, well-executed repair is the smartest thing you can do—if it’s done properly.

We provide fast, effective residential roof repairs for Wellington homes that have:

  • Hail or wind damage
  • Missing or lifted shingles
  • Minor leaks
  • Poor flashing or chimney sealing
  • Gutter-related roof drainage issues

Our trained team inspects, diagnoses, and fixes issues using quality materials and proven techniques. We never upsell you on something you don’t need, and we never leave behind half-done work.

Roofing Materials That Make Sense for Wellington Homes

Your home’s roof should match its style, its structure, and your goals as a homeowner. That’s why we offer a wide range of roofing materials—and explain the pros and cons of each clearly.

Asphalt Shingles – Reliable and Budget-Friendly

Ideal for many Wellington homes. These offer solid performance, are available in various styles and colors, and come with strong warranties.

Metal Roofing – Durability That Pays Off

Metal roofs offer unmatched lifespan, resistance to fire and wind, and energy efficiency. If you’re planning to stay in your home long-term, this is a great ROI.

Tile & Slate Roofing – Premium Aesthetics and Longevity

Want something timeless and luxurious? Tile and slate give you classic style and industry-leading durability. As certified installers with Ludowici and DaVinci, we know how to do it right.

Cedar Shake Roofing – Natural, Insulated, Unique

For homeowners looking for charm and performance, cedar shake provides great insulation and a beautiful, natural look that ages gracefully.

Flat Roofing – Modern and Functional

Perfect for low-slope areas and contemporary designs. We install flat roofing systems that are engineered for drainage and built to last.
