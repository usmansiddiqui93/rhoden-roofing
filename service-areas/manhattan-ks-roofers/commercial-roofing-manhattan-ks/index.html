FAQs About Commercial Roofing Services in Manhattan, KS

Before you move forward with a repair or replacement, you should have clear answers. As a trusted commercial roofing company providing services in Manhattan, here are the most common questions we hear from commercial property owners—and the straight answers.

How long does a commercial roof last in Manhattan, KS?

A commercial roof in Manhattan, KS typically lasts 20 to 40 years depending on the system, installation quality, and maintenance history. TPO, PVC, and metal systems perform well when installed by an experienced commercial roofing company and supported with routine commercial roofing service inspections after major hail or wind events. Kansas weather plays a major role in lifespan.

Should I repair or replace my commercial roof?

Whether you should repair or replace your commercial roof depends on its age and extent of damage. Minor membrane issues or isolated leaks can often be repaired, but widespread hail damage or recurring leaks usually make replacement the more cost-effective long-term decision.

How do I know if my commercial roof has hail damage?

You know your commercial roof has hail damage when there are visible membrane punctures, seam separation, flashing dents, or documented impact marks. The most reliable way to confirm damage is through a professional inspection with photo documentation for insurance.

Do you help with commercial roofing insurance claims?

Yes, as a full-service commercial roofing company, we assist with commercial roofing insurance claims. Our commercial roofing services include documenting damage, meeting with adjusters, and reviewing scope details to ensure the necessary repairs or full replacement are properly covered.

What warranties do you offer on commercial roofing in Manhattan?

We offer manufacturer-backed material warranties, including up to 50-year non-prorated coverage on qualifying systems, along with our Lifetime Workmanship Warranty. That means installation defects are covered for the life of the roof.

How soon should I schedule a commercial roof inspection after a storm?

You should schedule a commercial roof inspection as soon as possible after a storm. Early documentation protects your insurance claim timeline and prevents minor damage from developing into structural problems.
