Our Residential Roofing Services

We offer a full range of residential roofing services for homeowners in Colwich. Whether you’re dealing with leaks, storm damage, or just normal wear and tear, we’ve got you covered.

Roof Inspections

It all starts with a thorough roof inspection. We assess your entire roofing system—including shingles, flashing, gutters, and attic ventilation—and provide a detailed report outlining any current issues and potential future concerns.

Our inspections are:

  • Free for homeowners
  • Ideal after storms or before selling your home
  • Helpful for planning roof maintenance or upgrades

Roof Repairs

Small issues like missing shingles or flashing damage can quickly turn into bigger problems. We respond quickly to roof repair requests and focus on fixing the problem right the first time.

Common repair issues we handle:

  • Wind or hail damage
  • Minor leaks or water stains
  • Loose or missing shingles
  • Improper attic ventilation
  • Flashing and sealant problems

Full Roof Replacement

If your roof is over 15–20 years old or showing signs of major wear, it may be time for a roof replacement. We guide you through every step of the process—from selecting materials to coordinating cleanup.

Our team installs a variety of roofing systems, including:

  • Architectural asphalt shingles
  • Class 4 impact-resistant shingles
  • Metal roofing options
  • GAF-certified systems backed by extended warranties

We work efficiently to complete most residential roof replacements in one to two days, with minimal disruption to your daily routine.
