Home Design Trends: These Are the Most Popular Roof Colors Throughout Wichita

Oct 7, 20232 min readBy Rhoden Roofing
BlogWichita

Are you interested in giving your roofing an upgrade? The exterior of your home is one of the first things that people notice and renovations and changes are an important part of keeping your home fresh and presentable. Your home’s curb appeal plays a huge role in the value of your property. Maintaining a beautiful home goes beyond choosing a siding color or painting your front door. In this article, we’ll discuss 3 popular roof colors in Wichita that can take your roofing to the next level. 

The Best 3 Roof Colors in Wichita 

Before choosing a roofing color, think about the architectural style of your home and the overall aesthetic you are going for. Do you want a roofing color that adds a high contrast and is eye-catching, or are you looking for something more rustic and traditional? Deciding on the overall aesthetic you are going for will help you narrow down on the perfect residential roofing color for your home. 

Cool Greys & Blues

If you’re looking for a roofing color that is fresh, clean, and modern, blues and cool greys are a great option for you. These colors add a neutral, classic, harmonious, and sophisticated feel to your home that will blend into Wichita’s surroundings. Asphalt shingles are a popular roofing material that comes in these colors.

Warm Reds & Deep Browns 

Earthy browns and deep reds are a great roofing color choice for the homeowner who wants to add some warmth to their everyday living space. These colors are often used in rustic aesthetics and architecture and help add an inviting, cozy feel to your home and invoke a sense of tradition. These tones often are seen in tile roofing in Wichita. 

Dark Greys & Black

Another popular roofing color in Wichita is black and dark grey. These moody colors add an edgy and elegant feel to your home. The dark color of your roofing can create a striking and dimensional contrast against the color of your siding and any textures you choose to use on the exterior of your home. Ultimately, choosing a dark color for your roofing is a one-of-a-kind look that is eye-catching and sleek. You can often find slate roofing in these colors in the Wichita area.
