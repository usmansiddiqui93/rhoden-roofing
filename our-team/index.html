John Rhoden
Owner
View Profile

John got his start in the roofing business while working at ABC Supply Co, the largest distributor of roofing materials in the world, on his summers off from college at the University of San Diego. He developed his knowledge further while working as a project manager for a large Wichita roofing company. At that point, the Wichita native and East High graduate, decided to attend medical school at Creighton University. During his second year at Med school, he decided to choose a different path for himself and moved back to Wichita to start Rhoden Roofing in 2008.

John realized there was a great opportunity in the roofing industry to change the way business was done. By listening to the concerns his customers had and educating them about their project, John and his extremely capable team were able to quickly grow Rhoden Roofing into an award-winning company. He strives everyday to find a way to make the customer experience at Rhoden Roofing the best it can be. He found the best way to accomplish this was simple, listen to the customers needs and follow through every time. No Excuses!!
