Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I have to pay anything up front?

Nope. Your first payment can be deferred. That lets you get started without paying a penny until you’re ready.

What if my job ends up costing more than the original estimate?

Customers are typically approved for more than the project’s total cost. If your project changes, any unclaimed funds that you were approved for remain available until the project’s completion.

What happens if I apply but don't like the terms?

Prequalification just provides you with options. There’s no commitment, no risk, and no impact on your credit score.

Can I just finance a portion of the project?

Absolutely. Many customers combine financing with insurance or out-of-pocket funds. As long as it’s within the project’s scope and meets the $2,000 minimum, you can use as much or as little of your approved amount as you like.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes, it can be paid at any time with no extra fees. Whether it’s in 20 years or one week, it’s your schedule.

What if I need more than just a roof or additional funding?

No problem. For bigger projects, secured options with higher limits are often available that can cover all kinds of home improvements. It’s a great fit if you’re planning ahead or tackling multiples projects at once.

What's the difference between unsecured and secured lending?

Secured lending options require collateral, while unsecured lending options don’t. To learn more about the differences between unsecured and secured lending, read our article covering the key differences between them.
