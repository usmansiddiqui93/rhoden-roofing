Roof Replacement: Future-Proof Your Home

If your roof is 15 to 25 years old, it’s not a matter of “if” it will fail—it’s when. A full roof replacement is one of the smartest investments you can make in your property. It boosts curb appeal, improves energy efficiency, and most importantly, keeps your home protected.

How to Know You Need a New Roof

  • Your shingles are curling, cracking, or missing entirely
  • You’ve had multiple repairs over the past few years
  • Your attic shows signs of moisture or poor ventilation
  • The roof looks worn, sagging, or just plain tired
  • You want to upgrade your home before selling

We don’t just swap out old materials. We assess the full system—including underlayment, flashing, and ventilation—and install products that hold up to South Haven’s climate. We work with top-tier manufacturers like GAF, DaVinci, and Malarkey, and every project is backed by our Lifetime Workmanship Warranty.

Translation: This is the last roof you’ll ever need.

Roof Repairs: Handle Problems Before They Get Expensive

Not every issue calls for a full replacement. But small problems left unchecked? That’s how you end up with leaks, mold, and structural headaches.

That’s why we offer fast, thorough repair services that not only solve the immediate issue—but prevent future ones.

Common Repairs We Handle

  • Hail and wind damage
  • Shingle blow-offs and lifting
  • Active leaks or moisture intrusion
  • Flashing and chimney vulnerabilities
  • Gutter or drainage issues affecting roof performance

We always start with a complete inspection, so you get full visibility into what’s happening—not just a band-aid solution.

Roofing Materials That Match South Haven’s Needs

Every home is different. So is every roofing system. We’ll help you select the right material based on performance, appearance, and how long you plan to stay in your home.

Asphalt Shingles – Practical and Affordable

Still the most common choice in Kansas, asphalt shingles offer solid protection, a wide range of design options, and budget-friendly reliability.

Metal Roofing – Built for the Long Haul

Metal systems last for decades and offer energy efficiency, durability against wind and hail, and minimal maintenance.

Tile & Slate – High-End Durability and Curb Appeal

These classic materials offer premium protection and a timeless look—perfect for homeowners who want long-term performance and unique style.

Cedar Shake – Rustic Charm Meets Real Performance

Cedar shake offers natural insulation and weather resistance, with a distinct, beautiful aesthetic.

Flat Roofing – Streamlined for Modern Builds

Perfect for low-slope or modern designs, flat systems are built for efficient drainage and long-term reliability.
