Types of Residential Roofs We Install

At Rhoden Roofing, we take the time to understand your preferences and present the best options for your roof replacement. Our extensive selection of roofing types reflects our commitment to meeting your needs with personalized solutions.

Asphalt Shingle Roofs

Asphalt shingle roofing revitalizes your home’s safety and curb appeal. It’s a popular option that offers robust protection when dangerous weather hits Andover, affordability, and customization thanks to its accommodating selection of colors and styles.

Metal Roofs

Boost your home’s efficiency and protection with metal roofing. It’s prized for its lifespan of up to 50 years and impressive resistance to extreme weather. Its reflective properties can notably lower cooling costs. Metal roofing is ideal for homeowners who value long-term benefits.

Flat Roofs

Flat roofs present a world of possibilities. In addition to their sleek design, they create outdoor living spaces that can be utilized for any number of purposes, including solar panel installations, offering homeowners a solution for sustainability and energy savings.

Cedar Roofs

Cedar roofing infuses your home with natural beauty and charm. Its rich texture and color uplift your home’s appearance while its natural insulation helps regulate its temperature. As a renewable resource, it’s an ideal eco-friendly option.

Tile Roofs

The enduring strength and distinctive beauty of tile roofing are owed to its resistance to fading and deterioration. Tile roofing is a desirable feature for homebuyers because it has a lifespan of at least 50 years, giving you an advantage if you sell your home.

Slate Roofs

Slate roofs last more than 100 years and are unbeatable in longevity. Your slate roof will be unique because each tile has its own shade and texture. Extreme weather is no match for slate, keeping your home protected no matter what Mother Nature brings.
