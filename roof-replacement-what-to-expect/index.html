What to Expect During a Roof Replacement Project

Apr 3, 20231 min readBy Rhoden Roofing
BlogResidential RoofingWhat to Expect

You have an upcoming roof replacement project. This is a process that people (if they’re lucky) rarely go through. Understandably, you’re wondering exactly what to expect from the preparation and construction process. This article serves as an overview for what to expect from initial call and inspection all the way through construction, billing, and insurance claim assistance (if applicable). This is the main overview article for our “What to Expect” series, so

Note that the preparation and construction process will vary by contractor, property type, roof type, etc. This article is focused on single-family residential projects, and is based on our own construction process. Rhoden Roofing completes over 500 re-roofs per year in the greater Wichita, Kansas area. Just as every home is unique, so is every project. However, what to expect from the process remains consistent.
