Types of Asphalt Shingle Roofs We Install

There are three types of asphalt shingles to consider: three-tab asphalt shingles, architectural, and premium asphalt shingles.

Three-tab Asphalt Shingles

Three-tab shingles are the most common, classified by their cutouts, which make the look like there separate pieces. They’re affordable, lightweight, easy to maintain, energy efficient, and can last up to 20 years. 

Architectural Asphalt Shingles

Architectural shingles, or dimensional shingles, are a great choice if your home’s curb appeal is important. They come in more colors, are laminated, and add dimension to your roof’s appearance. They often come in reflective versions that help with cooling costs in the summer. They last up to 50 years. 

Premium Asphalt Shingles

Luxury shingles are among the top-of-the-line in every category of shingles. They’re thicker, heavier, and produce lines that create depth not typical in regular roofs. You can order them to look like natural cedar or slate.

The Pros and Cons of Asphalt Shingle Roofing

When making your decision about the type of roofing you prefer, there will be pros and cons. It’s best to know what to expect. 

Pros of Asphalt Shingles

  • Affordability: in conjunction with financing options, asphalt is a no-brainer for homeowners on a budget. With proper installation and maintenance, a quality roof doesn’t have to break the bank.
  • Color Variety: It comes in a wide variety of colors, ranging from neutrals to statement colors. Depending on how much you want to spend, you can even choose asphalt shingle roofing that emulates wood or slate.
  • Simple Installation: Asphalt shingle roof repair is simple and cost-effective. New shingles can sometimes be installed on top of existing shingles. With proper maintenance asphalt shingle roofing has a lifespan of up to 20 years.

Cons of Asphalt Shingles

  • Not Environmentally Friendly: Asphalt shingles are not recyclable since they can release harmful emissions. As the shortest roof lifespan, asphalt shingles contribute a lot of waste.
  • Susceptible to Weather: Wide-ranging temperatures and extreme weather conditions can cause cracks and warping of some asphalt shingle types.
