Ensuring Real Estate Transactions Go Smoothly

Whether we are helping to sell a property or helping the buyer, our role is the same: providing peace of mind about the roofing system.

Roofs are one of the major systems of a home, and with a big job to do keeping weather out. Because roofs are costly and leaks have such high consequences, they are one of the most-often negotiated components of a real estate transaction.

Our Process: Assisting With Real Estate Transactions

1) Realtors Become a Preferred Partner

  • Realtors apply into Preferred Partner Program
  • Limited Spots keep inspection availability open
  • One free annual roof tune-up for agent’s home or a customer’s home ($771 value)

2) Roof Inspections Completed

  • Completed prior to or at time of listing
  • Limited to 30-minute radius from Wichita
  • Single family and steep-roof properties
  • Any existing insurable damage must be filed w/ insurance prior to sale

3) Detailed Photo Condition Report

  • Checklist of all roof systems & components
  • Green/Yellow/Red rating on conditions
  • Details provided for appraiser, residential inspector
  • Bid issued on any maintenance recommended (Yellow) or repair/replacement recommendations (Red)

4) Repairs, Tune-up, or Replacement (If Needed)

  • Skip the scheduling queue if needed for closing timeline
  • 180-day bid terms & priority scheduling mean work needed can happen pre or post sale
  • 15% off repairs/replacements needed
  • 35% off roof tune-up for a $499 minimum

5) Our Promise and Deliverables

  • 12-Month Leak-Free Guarantee and Warranty Issued if Grade Allows
  • Final inspection report showing work completed
  • Yard sign
  • Aerial photos provided for listing
  • Listing language for roof condition
  • Warranty Work Provided to Buyer (Free) for 12 Months
  • Post-sale gift to buyer (replacements)

REALTORS: APPLY TO JOIN OUR PREFERRED PARTNER PROGRAM

To preserve our ability to react quickly and engage at the pace required for real estate transactions, we have limited spots available. Our partnership agreement is set at 12 months.
