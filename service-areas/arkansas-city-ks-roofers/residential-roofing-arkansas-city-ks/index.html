Your residential roof should last for decades, not just until the next hailstorm. Whether you need a full roof replacement or a few repairs to extend the life of your current roof, we provide expert support to get it done right the first time.

Roof Replacement: A Smart Investment in Protection

Replacing your roof isn’t just about fixing a problem—it’s about avoiding the next one. A high-quality roof replacement protects your home from water damage, improves insulation, and boosts resale value.

Signs It’s Time to Replace Your Roof

  • Your roof is over 20 years old
  • Shingles are cracked, curled, or missing
  • There’s visible damage from a recent storm
  • You’ve experienced frequent leaks or moisture inside
  • You want to improve your home’s energy efficiency

We use premium materials from brands like GAF, Malarkey, and DaVinci—products that are proven to perform in Kansas’s unpredictable weather. And because installation matters just as much as the materials, every replacement we do comes with a Lifetime Workmanship Warranty.

We’re not guessing. We’re building solutions that last.

Roof Repairs: Small Fixes Done Right

Not every issue calls for a full replacement. A leak in one spot or a few damaged shingles can be fixed quickly and efficiently—if it’s done by someone who knows what they’re doing.

Our Repair Services Cover

  • Hail and wind damage
  • Roof leaks and water stains
  • Shingle replacements
  • Flashing and chimney repairs
  • Ventilation and drainage issues

We start with a full inspection so we don’t just treat the symptoms—we find and fix the root cause.

Roofing Material Options That Fit Your Goals

Your roofing choice depends on more than just appearance. We help homeowners in Arkansas City choose materials based on lifespan, weather resistance, energy efficiency, and budget.

Asphalt Shingles – Practical & Popular

A solid, reliable choice for most homes. Cost-effective and available in a variety of colors and styles.

Metal Roofing – Built to Withstand Kansas Weather

If you want durability, low maintenance, and long-term energy savings, metal is a strong investment.

Tile & Slate – Classic Looks, Maximum Longevity

For homeowners who want something beautiful and built to last, tile and slate are premium options.

Cedar Shake – Timeless Charm and Natural Insulation

Cedar offers unique texture and appearance, plus excellent insulating qualities.

Flat Roofing – Smart, Sleek, and Effective

Perfect for modern homes or low-slope areas. Engineered to manage drainage and resist leaks.
