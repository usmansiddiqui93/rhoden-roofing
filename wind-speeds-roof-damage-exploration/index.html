How Fast is too Fast: Exploring Wind Speeds and Roof Damage

Jun 22, 20232 min readBy Rhoden Roofing
Residential RoofingCommon Causes of Roof DamageWichita
Living in Wichita, Kansas means being exposed to a range of weather conditions, including strong winds. The wind can pose a significant threat to your home, particularly your roof. To help you understand the impact of wind on your roof and how to protect it, we’ve prepared this detailed guide. For all your roofing needs, trust Rhoden Roofing, a reputable roofing company serving Wichita and the surrounding areas.

Understanding Wind Speeds

Before delving into the effects of wind on roofs, it’s essential to understand wind speeds and how they are measured. Wind speeds are typically measured in miles per hour (mph). Here are the classifications commonly used to categorize wind speeds:

  • Light Breeze: 1-3 mph
  • Gentle Breeze: 4-7 mph
  • Moderate Breeze: 8-12 mph
  • Fresh Breeze: 13-18 mph
  • Strong Breeze: 19-24 mph
  • Near Gale: 25-31 mph
  • Gale: 32-38 mph
  • Strong Gale: 39-46 mph
  • Storm: 47-54 mph
  • Violent Storm: 55-63 mph
  • Hurricane: 64+ mph

The Impact of Wind on Roofs

Roofs are designed to withstand certain wind speeds based on their construction and the materials used. However, high wind speeds can cause significant storm damage to roofs, compromising their integrity and leading to costly repairs or replacements.

  • Shingle Damage: Strong winds can lift and loosen shingles, causing them to curl, crack, or even tear off completely. Missing shingles expose your roof to potential leaks and further damage.
  • Roof Deck Damage: Intense winds can create uplift forces, leading to roof deck damage. This can result in gaps, sagging, or even partial roof collapses if not addressed promptly.
  • Water Infiltration: When wind lifts or damages shingles, it creates entry points for water to seep into your home. This can lead to interior water damage, mold growth, and structural issues.

Protecting Your Roof from Wind Damage

While you can’t control the weather, you can take steps to protect your Wichita home’s roof from wind damage:

  • Regular Inspections: Schedule regular roof inspections with a professional roofing company like Rhoden Roofing. They will assess the condition of your roof and identify any vulnerable areas that require attention.
  • Reinforce Vulnerable Areas: Reinforce the edges of your roof, such as the eaves and gables, with additional fasteners or adhesives to enhance their resistance to wind uplift.
  • Choose Wind-Resistant Materials: When installing or replacing your roof, opt for wind-resistant materials designed to withstand high wind speeds. Work with a trusted roofing contractor like Rhoden Roofing, who can guide you in selecting the right materials for your specific needs.
  • Maintain Proper Roof Ventilation: Adequate roof ventilation helps equalize air pressure, reducing the risk of wind-induced damage. Ensure your roof has proper vents and that they are free from blockages.
