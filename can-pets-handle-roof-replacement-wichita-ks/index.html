Can My Pets Handle a Roof Replacement?

What Homeowners Worry About Before Roof Replacement

Before roof replacement starts, most homeowners are not worried about shingles.
They are worried about what the job will actually be like.

How long does roof replacement take?
Should I stay home during roof replacement?
Will my dog freak out?
Will my cat hide all day?
Do companies that replace roofs work on the weekends?

We hear these questions all the time from homeowners across Wichita, KS, especially after hail storms when roof replacement has to be scheduled quickly.

Roof replacement is loud, but it should not feel chaotic. When the job is planned correctly and installed by an experienced crew, most homeowners and pets handle it without any problems.

In this guide, we’ll cover:

  • How long roof replacement takes in Wichita
  • Whether you should stay home during roof replacement
  • How dogs and cats usually react to the noise
  • Whether roofing companies work on weekends
  • How to prepare your home before installation
  • What makes roof replacement go smoothly

If you want roof replacement to go as stress-free as possible, the best thing you can do is know what to expect before the work starts.

What Roof Replacement Is Really Like for Homeowners in Wichita, KS

Roof replacement is one of the loudest projects you can have done on your home, but it is also one of the fastest when the crew knows what they are doing.

During a typical roof replacement in Wichita, you will hear:

  • Footsteps on the roof all day
  • Nail guns and hammering
  • Shingles being removed
  • Dump trailers filling up
  • Trucks coming and going

This is normal.

Most roof replacement jobs in Wichita, KS move quickly because weather can change fast. Crews work efficiently to remove the old roof, inspect the decking, install underlayment, and complete the new roofing system as fast as possible without cutting corners.

Because roof replacement usually only lasts a day or two, many homeowners are surprised that the noise does not last nearly as long as they expected. That is why one of the first questions we hear is how long roof replacement takes and whether they should stay home during the work.

How Long Does Roof Replacement Take in Wichita, KS?

One of the most common questions we hear before roof replacement starts is how long does roof replacement take.

We complete most roof replacements in under one day. Larger homes, steep roofs, or repairs under the shingles can make the job take longer, but most projects are finished quickly once the crew starts.

If you are wondering how long does it take to replace a roof, the biggest factors are:

  • Size of the roof
  • Pitch and complexity
  • Weather conditions
  • Decking repairs
  • Number of layers removed
  • Material type

Storm damage can also affect how long roof replacement takes, especially if insurance inspections or supplements are needed.

We explain the full process in our complete guide to roof replacement in Kansas, including what happens before installation, during tear-off, and after the job is finished. For a behind-the-scenes look at what happens between signing your contract and the first shingle coming off, check out a walkthrough for Rhoden Roofing’s process here.

Knowing how long roof replacement takes helps homeowners plan for work schedules, pets, and whether they want to stay home during the job.

Should I Stay Home During Roof Replacement?

Many homeowners ask if they should stay home during roof replacement, and the answer is simple. You can stay home, but you do not have to.

Roof replacement is loud. There will be constant movement on the roof, hammering, and debris being removed. Some people are fine staying home, while others prefer to leave for the day, especially if they work from home or have pets that get nervous.

If you are asking should I stay home during roof replacement, think about:

  • How sensitive you are to noise
  • How long roof replacement will take
  • Whether you work from home
  • How your pets react to loud sounds
  • How comfortable you are with crews outside all day

Many homeowners in Wichita stay home the first day, then leave the second day once they realize how loud the work can be. Others plan to be gone the entire time.

Either way is fine as long as the contractor has access to the roof and driveway.

How Roof Replacement Affects Dogs, Cats, and Other Pets

Pets usually react more to roof replacement than homeowners do.

Dogs may bark at the noise.
Cats may hide.
Some animals get nervous when strangers are around the house.

This is completely normal during roof replacement, especially during tear-off.

If you have pets at home during roof replacement, a few simple steps help a lot:

  • Keep pets inside
  • Use a quiet room if possible
  • Make sure gates stay closed
  • Tell the crew you have pets
  • Consider boarding if your pet gets anxious

Most roof replacement jobs only last a short time, which is why knowing how long roof replacement takes makes planning easier for pet owners.

A good rule of thumb: if your dog barks or is stressed when it hears a knock at the door, it will bark or be stressed by a roof replacement.

Do Companies That Replace Roofs Work on the Weekends in Wichita?

Another question we hear often is do companies that replace roofs work on the weekends.

Most roof replacement projects in Wichita, KS are scheduled during the week, but weekend work sometimes happens when schedules get backed up after storms.

Roofing companies may work weekends when:

  • Storm damage creates a long wait list
  • Weather delays during the week
  • Insurance deadlines
  • Homeowner requests weekend work

Some neighborhoods or HOAs limit weekend construction, so the schedule may depend on local rules.

If timing matters, ask your contractor early how long roof replacement will take and whether weekend work is possible.

How to Prepare Your Home and Pets Before Roof Replacement

A little preparation makes roof replacement much easier.

We answer this question in-depth in another article, but some key takeaways can be found below.

  • Move vehicles out of the driveway
  • Secure pets inside the home
  • Remove fragile items from walls
  • Cover items in the attic
  • Let neighbors know work is scheduled
  • Plan where you will be during the day
  • Mow the yard
  • Move patio and breakable outdoor decor

Most roof replacement projects only last a short time, but knowing how long roof replacement takes helps you decide whether to stay home, leave for the day, or make plans for pets.

Good preparation makes the job feel much less stressful.

Why a Professional Roof Replacement Should Not Feel Stressful

Roof replacement is a big project, but it should not feel out of control.

When the job is done by an experienced crew, the process follows a clear plan:

  1. Inspection
  2. Scheduling
  3. Material delivery
  4. Tear-off
  5. Installation
  6. Cleanup
  7. Final check

At Rhoden Roofing, we install roof replacement projects across Wichita every week, and the goal is always the same — finish the job quickly, keep the site clean, and make sure the homeowner knows exactly what to expect.

When homeowners understand how long roof replacement takes and how the job will be done, the process feels much easier.

Common Questions Homeowners Ask Before Roof Replacement in Wichita, KS

Before roof replacement starts, homeowners usually want to know how long the job will take, whether they should stay home, and how the noise will affect pets. These are the most common questions we hear from homeowners in Wichita, KS when scheduling roof replacement.

How long does roof replacement take?

We complete most roof replacements in one day, from tear-off to final cleanup. Roofs larger than 50 squares, an exceptionally steep pitch, or a cut-up roof with many unique angles and corners may two days or more.

Should I stay home during roof replacement?

You can stay home during roof replacement, but many homeowners choose to leave because the noise can be constant during tear-off and installation. If you work from home or have pets that get nervous, it may be easier to be gone while the roof replacement is in progress.

Will my pets be okay during roof replacement?

Most pets are okay during roof replacement, but dogs and cats can get stressed from the noise and movement on the roof. Keeping pets inside, using a quiet room, or boarding them for the day can make roof replacement easier for both the pet and the homeowner.

Do companies that replace roofs work on the weekends?

Some companies that replace roofs do work on the weekends, but most roof replacement jobs in Wichita are scheduled during the week. Weekend work may happen after storms, during busy seasons, or when weather delays make the schedule tight.

Is roof replacement loud inside the house?

Roof replacement is loud inside the house, especially during the tear-off stage when shingles are removed. The noise usually lasts one to two days, which is why many homeowners ask how long roof replacement takes before scheduling the job.

How should I prepare my home before roof replacement starts?

You should prepare your home before roof replacement by moving cars, securing pets, protecting attic items, and knowing the schedule ahead of time. When homeowners know how long roof replacement will take, planning around the work becomes much easier.
