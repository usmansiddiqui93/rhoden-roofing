Commercial Roofing Systems We Install

No two commercial buildings are the same. That’s why we offer multiple system options—each one selected based on what works best for your building, climate exposure, and long-term ROI.

TPO Roofing: Energy-Efficient and Budget-Friendly

TPO is ideal for flat or low-slope commercial roofs in Junction City. It’s UV-resistant, durable, and highly cost-effective over the life of the roof. We install fully adhered and mechanically fastened TPO systems with heat-welded seams for leak-proof protection.

PVC Roofing: For Harsh Environments and High Traffic

PVC is your go-to for rooftops exposed to chemicals, grease, or frequent foot traffic—like restaurants, industrial sites, or hospitals. With welded seams and chemical resistance, it’s built for tough environments.

Metal Roofing: Maximum Durability for Kansas Weather

Metal roofing systems (like standing seam or R-panel) offer unmatched durability against hail, high winds, and freeze-thaw cycles. If you’re thinking long-term, this is it.

Asphalt Shingles: Great for Steep-Slope Commercial Applications

Not every commercial building has a flat roof. For office parks, HOAs, churches, and community centers with pitched roofs, we install high-impact, Class 4 architectural shingles.

Tile, Slate & Synthetic: For Buildings with Architectural Impact

Looking to make a visual statement? We’re certified by DaVinci Roofscapes and Ludowici to install premium synthetic slate and natural tile systems that combine performance with prestige.

Answers to Common Commercial Roofing Questions in Junction City

Managing a commercial property comes with plenty of decisions—and even more unknowns. Below, we answer the most common questions business owners and property managers in Junction City ask us about commercial roofing systems, lifespan, maintenance, and warranty expectations.

How long does a commercial roof last?

A commercial roof typically lasts 20 to 30 years, depending on the roofing material, installation quality, and maintenance schedule. TPO and PVC systems often last two to three decades with proper care, while metal roofs can exceed 40 years. At Rhoden Roofing, we also offer preventive maintenance programs to extend the life of your commercial roof in Junction City.

What are the signs that a commercial roof needs replacement?

Signs your commercial roof needs replacement include persistent leaks, standing water, cracked or shrinking membranes, and rising energy bills. If your roof is more than 20 years old and repairs are becoming frequent, it’s likely more cost-effective to consider full replacement. Our team provides transparent inspections and detailed reporting so you can make a confident decision.

What’s the best roofing system for flat commercial buildings?

The best roofing system for flat commercial buildings in Junction City depends on your building type and use case. TPOis the most popular due to its durability and energy efficiency. PVC is ideal for environments exposed to grease or chemicals, like restaurants and labs. We evaluate your building’s needs and budget to recommend the system with the best return on investment.

Do commercial roofs come with a warranty?

Yes, commercial roofs come with both material warranties and workmanship warranties. Rhoden Roofing offers up to a 50-year non-prorated material warranty (depending on system) and a Lifetime Workmanship Warranty on qualifying full roof replacements. These warranties offer peace of mind and long-term protection for your investment.

Does Rhoden Roofing work around business hours and tenant schedules?

Yes, Rhoden Roofing works around your business hours and tenant schedules to minimize disruption. We create a custom project plan that fits your operational needs—whether that means weekend work, early morning crews, or quiet hours for medical or retail environments.
