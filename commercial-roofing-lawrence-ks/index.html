Roofing Systems We Install for Lawrence Commercial Properties

We don’t believe in cookie-cutter roofing. Different properties demand different solutions, and we’re trained in every major commercial roofing system.

TPO Roofing

TPO is a great fit for many flat-roofed commercial buildings. It’s durable, energy-efficient, and budget-conscious. Bonus: its reflective properties reduce your building’s cooling load in the summer.

PVC Roofing

If your building handles oils, grease, or harsh chemicals—think restaurants or manufacturing—PVC offers chemical resistance and long-lasting waterproofing.

Metal Roofing

Metal roofing is one of the most durable systems we install. Ideal for steep-slope commercial buildings, churches, and institutions, it offers excellent weather resistance and long lifespan with minimal upkeep.

Roof Coatings

Roof coatings can extend the life of an aging roof without the cost of full replacement. If your existing roof is structurally sound, this may be the most cost-effective upgrade available.

Architectural Shingles (for steep-slope or mixed-use buildings)

For buildings that blend commercial and residential features, such as multi-family housing or offices in converted homes, architectural shingles offer a balance of style, durability, and cost-efficiency.

Smart Answers to Common Commercial Roofing Questions in Lawrence, KS

If you manage or own commercial property in Lawrence, knowing what to expect from your roofing contractor isn’t just helpful—it’s essential. Below are answers to the most common questions we hear from local businesses before starting a project.

How much does a commercial roof cost in Lawrence?

A commercial roof in Lawrence typically costs between $8 and $14 per square foot, depending on the system you choose and your building’s complexity. TPO and PVC systems are generally more cost-efficient than metal or custom membrane builds, and costs also depend on local code compliance and substrate conditions.

How long will it take to install or replace my commercial roof?

Most commercial roofing projects in Lawrence are completed within 1 to 2 weeks, depending on size, scope, and weather. We provide detailed timelines before the job starts and maintain communication throughout the process to minimize business disruption.

What roofing system is best for commercial buildings in Kansas?

The best roofing system for commercial buildings in Kansas is typically TPO or PVC for flat roofs, and standing seam metal for sloped structures. These materials hold up well in Kansas weather, offer strong energy efficiency, and come with long-term manufacturer warranties.

Can you install commercial roofing around ongoing business operations?

Yes, we can install commercial roofing while your business remains open, using safety zones and phased scheduling. Our crews are trained to work around operational hours, reduce noise when possible, and maintain site cleanliness throughout the project.

How do I know if I need roof replacement or just repairs?

You may need roof replacement instead of repairs if your roof is older than 15 years, has multiple leaks, or shows membrane deterioration. A professional inspection is the only way to know for sure—Rhoden Roofing LLC provides full diagnostic assessments to guide your decision.

Do you offer emergency commercial roof repair in Lawrence?

Yes, we offer emergency commercial roof repair in Lawrence for issues like storm damage, active leaks, or structural concerns. Our team responds quickly, assesses the situation, and performs immediate fixes or temporary stabilization until a full repair can be scheduled.

Are you certified to install commercial roofing systems?

Yes, Rhoden Roofing LLC is certified by major manufacturers like GAF, Mule-Hide, GenFlex, and others to install commercial roofing systems. This certification ensures your roof qualifies for extended material warranties and meets strict installation standards.
